Method and limits
  • Working capital: scenario balances are rebuilt from days (DIO, DSO, DPO) on scenario revenue and COGS. Cash impact is minus the change in inventory + receivables − payables.
  • FX: the disclosed net USD and EUR monetary positions are revalued at spot × (1 + shock). The hedged share is locked at a forward derived from covered interest parity on TCMB deposit rates — a proxy, since free TRY forward quotes do not exist.
  • Funding cost: net trade working capital × TRY commercial loan rate × days / 365.
  • The FX-linked share of COGS is an illustrative assumption; Mavi does not disclose it. Set it to 0% to switch that channel off.
  • Balances are quarter-end points, not averages, and Mavi's business is seasonal. Inventory and payables are assumed to reprice immediately.
  • A simulation on public filings (KAP) and TCMB EVDS data. Not a forecast, not company guidance, not investment advice.
